Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about buying, renting, and listing properties on OurHomeIndia.

How does OurHomeIndia verify property listings?

Our audit team physically inspects property sites, confirms owner authenticity, checks municipal sanction maps, and reviews registry records before granting the "Verified" badge.

Can I obtain a bank loan on builder floors & villas?

Yes, we facilitate home loans up to 80-85% through our lending partners (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, PNB) with complete end-to-end documentation assistance.

What regions are covered by OurHomeIndia?

We feature premium verified listings across Delhi NCR (South Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Okhla) and key metropolitan corridors across India.

How can property owners list their properties?

Owners can submit their property details for free via our "Post Property" portal. Our team will verify and publish your listing within 24 hours.
